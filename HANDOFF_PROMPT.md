## How the project works
- **One HTML file.** The whole game is `src/index.html`, using three.js **r128** global builds from CDNs:
  - three.min.js from cdnjs
  - GLTFLoader.js and SkeletonUtils.js from jsdelivr `three@0.128.0/examples/js`
- **Building.** `python3 build.py` embeds the car and character `.glb` models (base64 in `/*MODEL_DATA*/{}`) and the menu art JPEGs (in `/*ART_CSS*/`). The result is `dist/index.html`, about 6.7 MB and self-contained. Always edit `src/index.html`, never `dist/`.
- **Blender models.** Everything is generated by Python scripts using `bpy` (installed via pip, headless) in `blender/`:
  - `cars.py`: saloon, sports, van and police cars, with door hinge nodes `*_door_l` / `*_door_r` and right-hand-drive interiors. Saloon and police are 4-doors (also `*_door_bl` / `*_door_br`); the van has two back doors (`van_door_bl` / `van_door_br`) and four bench seats in the back. Run `python3 cars.py norender` to export only. Blender comes from `pip install bpy`.
  - `human.py`: rigged male and female characters with every wardrobe piece as a separate toggleable mesh. `python3 human.py exportonly` exports both.
  - `facepaint.py`: the cartoon face painter. The game paints faces at runtime with the same layout.
  - `wardrobe.json`: the outfit table (tops, bottoms, shoes, hats, eyewear, extras), shared by the game and the previews.
  - `beard_styles.json`: 30 beard styles.
  - Preview renderers: `clothes_preview.py`, `beard_grid.py` and others.

  The scripts use absolute paths like `/home/claude/blender/...`. Fix those for your environment first.

### Code map of `src/index.html`
The sections are in order and each starts with a `// ----` or `// ====` header:
- **Core and world:**
  - helpers, layout, renderer and materials
  - map canvas (ground texture and minimap) and collision
  - the city: a fictional UK city about 500 m across, generated by `src/city.js` (`buildCity()`; `build.py` inlines it at `/*CITY_JS*/`, and it runs in node too, for previews and tests). Districts: High Street centre with three roundabouts (`RW`, `C` = Market Cross, `RE`), Victorian terraces (NW), Victoria Park and Georgian crescents (NE), tower estate (SE), mills and works (SW), a ring road and a belt of buildings outside it. The street plan is the `NODES` / `EDGES` tables at the top of `city.js`.
  - `blocks` are the areas between roads (`poly`, `kerb`, `line` = building line, `walk` = pavement loop, `links` = zebra crossings to other blocks, `district`, `cx,cz`); the last one is the outer belt (`outer`). `corners(b)` returns `b.walk`; `blockAt(x,z)` / `blockIdxAt` find a block.
  - Traffic follows the road graph (`REDGES` / `RNODES`, left-hand lanes in `E.lanes[1|-1]`): `navTarget()`, `junctionPath()` (clockwise round roundabouts, give way to the right), `roadSpot()` for spawning, `resyncNav()`.
  - Collision is rotated boxes on a 16 m grid: `addColBox`, `colNear`, `colInside`, `rayCol`. Buildings are merged per material (`addBlockBuilding`, `finishChunks`); fronts are canvas-drawn textures (terraces, Georgian, shops + pub, towers, works).
  - Street furniture is instanced: Belisha beacons, phone boxes, post boxes, bus shelters.
- **Characters:**
  - rigged humans
  - wardrobe and `applyLook(h, look)`
  - runtime face painter (canvas texture with `flipY=false`)
- **Vehicles:** cars, and car occupants with door animation and enter/exit/jack sequences (`startSeq`, `seatActor`, `unseat`). The driver sits on the right; side `'l'` is the passenger seat. Seats and doors per car type live in `LAYOUTS` (seat keys `d`, `p`, `bl`, `br`, van `b1`–`b4`); every crew member takes their own free seat (`crewSeatFor`). Anyone walking to a door routes round the car (`aroundCar`), and following crew route round any car in the way (`steerPast`). Cabins are sized so every hairstyle and hat fits in every seat; seated legs use the `carLegs` overlay so feet stay in the footwell. If you change seats or roofs, re-check head clearance (test: raycast up from every seated head vertex). Hair caps and hats get the same 'rounder back of skull' bulge as the head (in `cap()`), so the skull never pokes through. Beard pieces are placed on the real head mesh (`head_bvh` in `surf()`) with their back edge just under the skin; full beards and chops are solid, smooth-edged sheets (`shell_piece`).
- **Game systems:**
  - peds and player
  - particles and audio (all synthesised with WebAudio, no sound files)
  - input and game logic
  - updates: the ped AI is `updatePed`, cop AI and traffic AI is `aiUpdate`
  - helicopter, roadblocks, pickups, spray shop, missions, camera, HUD
  - world population
- **Interface:**
  - settings (graphics low/medium/high; phones default to low)
  - touch controls
  - menu music
  - screens: loading → menu → game ↔ pause
- **Shops and weapons:**

  - weapons: 40 guns, slot inventory, ammo and reloads, and projectiles (rocket, grenades, molotov fire, tear gas, sticky bombs, flamethrower)
  - two-bone arm IK (`holdWeapon`, `ikArm`), which puts guns properly in both hands for the player and NPCs
  - gun shop "Lock & Load": a walk-in interior placed far off-map at (-720, -720), with a counter and the wall display

### Conventions and gotchas
- **Bone rotation.** `setBone(h, name, x, y, z)` sets rest × euler.
  - On bones that point down, +x swings the bone end backward.
  - On spine and head, +x leans forward, and head y turns the head.
  - Arm outward is -z for the left arm and +z for the right.
  - Overlays (aiming, phone poses and so on) are applied **after** `mixer.update`.
- **Weapon models** are built in code from merged primitives, one draw call each (`GB()` and `GDEF`). The model frame is +z forward (barrel), +y up, with the grip at the origin. `SUP[id]` gives the left-hand support point.
- **Wardrobe.** Visibility comes from the `WARD` tables in `wardrobe.json`. The colour tokens `top/skin/trim/white/bot` drive the materials `shirt, shoulder, sleeve, pants, shin, jacket, trim, skirt`.
- **City generation** is deterministic: `city.js` has its own seeded random, and building looks use another (`LR`). Don't add calls to the game's `rnd()` before the city is built, or plots and colours shift. Place new props deterministically, as the benches are. Turf saves (`blockrunner_turf`) are per block; a changed block count resets them.
- **Saves.** Progress is stored in localStorage under these keys:
  - `blockrunner_settings`
  - `blockrunner_look`
  - `blockrunner_money`
  - `blockrunner_arsenal`
  - `blockrunner_respect`
  - `blockrunner_turf`

  Keep old saves working. Normalise them on load; `normLook` shows how.
- **Mobile performance.** Watch draw calls and skinned meshes. Distant peds already animate at a lower rate. Keep the build size reasonable; it's currently 6.7 MB, mostly the two character `.glb` files.

## Police depth (van, spike strips, searchlight)
- **Police van** (`policevan`): `blender/cars.py` builds it from the van with Battenburg checks (`bsecs/brows/bskip/bx` config; checks start at the cab door and stay below the window sill (z<=1.12) so nothing covers glass or bonnet), a roof light bar and POLICE on the bonnet. In game it seats driver, passenger and 2-4 officers on the back benches. One spawns at 3+ stars (50% chance per patrol spawn, only one at a time) and one sits in every roadblock at 3+ stars. `unloadCops(c)` retries every frame so officers sharing a back door leave one after another.
- **Roadblocks** now start at 3 stars (max 2, only while you're in a car); the helicopter still comes at 4.
- **Spike strips** (`makeSpike`, `updateSpikes`) lie 13 m in front of each roadblock across your lane and most of the road. Driving over one sets `car.flat=30`: top speed x0.55, wobbling steering, sparks. The Spray Shop clears it, as does swapping cars.
- **Helicopter searchlight** (`heliLight`): additive cone plus a lit disc on the ground that chases you with a lag. While it's on you (within 7.5 m, with line of sight) your wanted level can't cool down (`lastSeen` is refreshed). Hide under a roof or break line of sight to shake it.

## Safehouse (Flat 14, Grove Court)
- The flat is a door on the front of the Grove Street tower block nearest the centre (chosen in `HOMELOT`, built in `placeHome`, a cyan ring plus minimap icon). Walk into the ring on foot with no wanted level to go in. It's a walkable room far off the map (`HX,HZ`, like the gun shop room); `INSIDE.type==='home'` routes to `homeUpdate`, and `ROOMC` holds the current room bounds for `insideBlocked`.
- **Bed** (`bedMenu`): Save game and Sleep. Saving sets `HOMEDATA.saved`; after that you start the game outside the flat and respawn there after WASTED/BUSTED. Money, weapons, respect, turf and your look already auto-save on every change, so Save is mostly the respawn point and a summary.
- **Wardrobe** (`wardrobeMenu`): up to 6 saved outfits with live preview. Anything bought at Threads or the barber is added automatically (`homeAddOutfit`).
- **Lock-up keys** (`keysMenu`): park the last car you drove within 45 m of the door (not police cars), up to 4 stored; bring one round and it appears at the nearest free kerb (`roadNear`) flagged `c.keep` so the cleanup never deletes it. Stored data is type, colour, hp.
- All of it lives in `localStorage['blockrunner_home']` as `{saved,at,outfits[],garage[]}`. Old saves have no key and behave as before.
- `lmOpen/lmClose/lmRender` are a small reusable list menu (state `'list'`) that borrows the shop panel; rows can have `act`, `onSel` (live preview) and `del`.
